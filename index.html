The Experience

What Ki means,
and why it's the name

Ki (気) is the Japanese word for vital energy — the current that moves through a body when nothing is blocking it. Everything here is built to clear the blockage and let it move again.

That's why we don't simply arrive and start. Every session follows the same four movements, whether you're on our table or your own.

Reserve Your Session

  1. Consultation

    A short intake — where it hurts, how you sleep, what your work does to your shoulders. Pressure and focus are agreed before hands touch skin.

  2. Preparation

    We transform your space: professional table, warmed linens, dimmed light, essential oils, and a curated sound bath. Roughly fifteen minutes.

  3. Treatment

    Uninterrupted bodywork built around your intake — never a script. Full professional draping throughout, always.

  4. Integration

    Water, a few minutes of quiet, and two or three specific movements to hold the release. Emailed to you afterward so nothing is forgotten.

Good to Know

Before your first session

What do I need to prepare at home?

Almost nothing. We need a clear space roughly 7 × 9 feet — a living room or bedroom works perfectly — and access to a power outlet. We bring the table, linens, oils, music, and everything else. Please have a bathroom available and, if you have pets, somewhere comfortable for them during the session.

Is this appropriate and professional?

Completely. K.I Therapy is a professional massage practice — fully qualified and fully insured. Every session uses professional draping at all times: you are covered by a sheet throughout, and only the area being worked on is ever uncovered. We are strictly a therapeutic practice, and any request outside that scope ends the session immediately with no refund.

How do I book, and how do I pay?

Book online in about sixty seconds, or call +44 7871 141090. A card deposit holds your appointment and is applied to your total. The balance is due at the session by card, tap, or transfer. Gratuity is already included in every price — please don't feel you need to add anything.

What's your cancellation policy?

Life happens. Reschedule or cancel free up to 24 hours before your appointment. Inside 24 hours, the deposit is retained — for mobile sessions, the travel and the blocked time are already committed by then. No-shows are charged in full.

Should I choose 60, 90, or 120 minutes?

For a specific complaint — one shoulder, low back, neck — 60 minutes in-studio is enough. For full-body work, 90 is the honest minimum, which is why in-residence sessions start there: by the time we've set up, a 60-minute mobile session isn't fair to either of us. 120 minutes is for when you want to stop counting.

Do you work with injuries or medical conditions?

Often, yes — but it depends. Please mention any injury, surgery, pregnancy, blood-clotting condition, or medication at booking so we can adjust the plan or, when appropriate, ask for clearance from your physician first. Massage is a complement to medical care, never a replacement for it.

Can I combine massage and personal training?

That's the whole idea, and it's what makes K.I different. Bodywork releases the restriction; training keeps it from coming back. Most clients settle into training weekly and bodywork every two to four weeks — Ki Circle members get member pricing across both.
